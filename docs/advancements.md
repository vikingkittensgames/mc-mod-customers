## Advancements

The Customers mod includes a broad set of advancements for building a shop and serving customers.
It also records player statistics and integrates with FTB Quests, giving players and modpack
authors several ways to track progress and create goals.

![Customers advancement tree]({{ '/screenshots/advancements.png' | relative_url }})

## Serving Advancements

Serving advancements track work with customers, shifts, leaderboards, and Suppliers. An item served
counts a completed requested-item transaction, rather than every individual item in the sold stack.

| Advancement | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Day Shift | Serve a requested item during a Day Shift. |
| Night Shift | Serve a requested item during a Night Shift. |
| Breakfast Shift | Serve a requested item during a Breakfast Shift. |
| Lunch Shift | Serve a requested item during a Lunch Shift. |
| Dinner Shift | Serve a requested item during a Dinner Shift. |
| First Item Served | Complete one requested-item transaction with a customer. |
| 50 Items Served | Complete 50 requested-item transactions with customers. |
| 100 Items Served | Complete 100 requested-item transactions with customers. |
| 500 Items Served | Complete 500 requested-item transactions with customers. |
| 1000 Items Served | Complete 1,000 requested-item transactions with customers. |
| 100 Pet Items Served | Complete 100 requested pet-item transactions. |
| 100 Casual Customers Served | Serve 100 distinct casual customers. |
| 100 Normal Customers Served | Serve 100 distinct normal customers. |
| 100 Impatient Customers Served | Serve 100 distinct impatient customers. |
| One-Star Shift | Finish a shift with at least one star. |
| Two-Star Shift | Finish a shift with at least two stars. |
| Three-Star Shift | Finish a shift with at least three stars. |
| Four-Star Shift | Finish a shift with at least four stars. |
| Five-Star Shift | Finish a shift with five stars. |
| Leader | Take the lead on a Customer Leaderboard containing scores for multiple players. |
| Supplies Purchased | Complete one Supplier purchase. |

## Building Advancements

Building advancements follow the process of crafting, configuring, and expanding a Customers shop.

| Advancement | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Customer Spawner Crafted | Craft a Customer Spawner. |
| Customer Spawner Configured | Configure at least one item for customers to buy. |
| Multiple Levels Configured | Configure at least one sell item on a Customer Spawner level after the first. |
| Customers Pets | Configure a Customer Spawner with a pet percentage above zero and at least one sell item. |
| Customer Counter Placed | Place a block matching the configured counter near a Customer Spawner. |
| Supplier Spawner Crafted | Craft a Supplier Spawner. |
| Supplier Spawner Configured | Configure at least one item for a Supplier to sell. |
