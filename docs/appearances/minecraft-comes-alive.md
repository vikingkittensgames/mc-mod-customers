### Minecraft Comes Alive Appearance

![mod-logo-mca-reborn.png]({{ '/screenshots/mod-logo-mca-reborn.png' | relative_url }})
The [Minecraft Comes Alive](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecraft-comes-alive-reborn) appearance is supplied by the optional Customers MCA Reborn Appearance add-on for Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric and NeoForge, with MCA Reborn version 7.7.9 or newer. Install Customers, Architectury API, MCA Reborn, and the matching appearance add-on build on both the server and client. The appearance then becomes available in Customer and Supplier Spawners. It uses
MCA's human villager models, genetics, skin layers, clothing, hairstyles, and
configured villager voices, including its yes and no trade responses, while
preserving normal Customer and Supplier behavior, including sitting while
waiting. The saved appearance variation keeps each villager's MCA appearance
consistent. Customers and Suppliers using this appearance are always rendered
as adults. Existing worlds keep their MCA appearance selections because the
add-on registers the original `customers:mca` appearance identifier. The
Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge build and Minecraft 1.21.11 builds do not include this
add-on.
