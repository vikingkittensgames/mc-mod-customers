## Statistics

The Customers mod includes statistics for countable player events. These lifetime player totals are
used by Customers advancements and can also be observed by integrations such as FTB Quests.

![Player Stats]({{ '/screenshots/stats.png' | relative_url }})

| Statistic | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Customers: Items Served | Completed requested-item transactions with customers. |
| Customers: Pet Items Served | Completed transactions that serve an item to a customer's pet. |
| Customers: Shifts Finished | Customer Spawner shifts completed by the player. |
| Customers: Customers Served | Distinct customers served by the player. |
| Customers: Casual Customers Served | Distinct casual customers served by the player. |
| Customers: Normal Customers Served | Distinct normal customers served by the player. |
| Customers: Impatient Customers Served | Distinct impatient customers served by the player. |
| Customers: Supplies Purchased | Completed Supplier purchase transactions. |
